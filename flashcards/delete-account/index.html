Account deletion

Delete your Learn Chinese account

This page is for Learn Chinese: New HSK 2026, provided by China Essentials (developer: Joshua K. Johnson). You can request permanent deletion here even if you no longer have the app installed.

Permanent action. Account deletion removes your account and associated learning information. It cannot be undone. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not submit a deletion request.

Request deletion without the app

  1. From the email address associated with your Learn Chinese account, email support@chinaessentials.app.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Learn Chinese account and include the account email address. Do not send your password or a one-time sign-in code.
  3. We will reply to confirm the request and may ask you to verify control of the account email before processing it. We will not ask for your password.
  4. After verification, we will submit the account for permanent deletion and confirm when deletion is complete or explain if an issue prevents it.
Email a deletion request

Delete from the app

If you can access the app, open Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Review the warning, select the confirmation checkbox and press Delete Account. The in-app flow requires an internet connection and authenticated server confirmation. If the server does not confirm deletion, the app reports an error and preserves local account data so you can retry.

Information deleted

Once deletion is confirmed, the Learn Chinese account in Supabase Auth and associated personal records are deleted, including:

  • account login information and account identity;
  • profile details, such as display name and learning-level preferences;
  • learning progress, flashcard and grammar progress, saved vocabulary, achievements, study activity and statistics;
  • personal vocabulary decks, custom cards and imported personal vocabulary;
  • study sessions, resume state, assessment results and mock-test attempts; and
  • account-scoped sync and learning-access records, including associated premium account records.

Official HSK vocabulary, grammar, shared test content and other users’ records are not deleted. A profile photo selected in the app is stored locally; confirmed deletion clears that account’s photo and other account-scoped local files on the device used for deletion. If you requested deletion by email, uninstall the app or clear its storage on each device to remove local-only copies there.

Retention

The current deletion implementation has no configured retention exception for account learning records: they are removed as part of confirmed deletion. Support correspondence about the request may be retained only as reasonably necessary to document and respond to the request, protect service security, resolve a dispute or comply with a legal obligation. Copies in routine service-provider backups may persist temporarily until those backups rotate. Apple or Google may separately retain store or download records under their own policies.
