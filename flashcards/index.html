Personal vocabulary decks

Save words, build custom decks and import supported Pleco vocabulary files for focused review.

02

Hear it, then try it yourself.

Play a reference pronunciation with the speaker control, then use the separate microphone control to record your Mandarin and request pronunciation feedback. Results can include an overall score and separate sound and tone details.

Availability is limited to accounts enabled for this feature. A signed-in account, internet access and microphone permission are required.

03

Bring your Pleco bookmarks into My Decks.

Export bookmarked flashcards from Pleco as a supported TXT or XML file. On iOS, choose Learn Chinese in Pleco’s share options; you can also import a saved export file. Preview the cards, name the deck, and choose whether to keep categories as tags, split them into decks, or ignore them before importing to My Decks.
