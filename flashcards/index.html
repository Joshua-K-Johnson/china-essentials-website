From Pleco to practice

Turn your Pleco bookmarks into a flashcard deck you can study.

Pleco
Learn Chinese

  1. 01 Export

    Export from Pleco

    Under Bookmarks, choose Import / Export. Pick TXT or XML.

  2. 02 Share

    Send to Learn Chinese

    Tap Export and choose Learn Chinese in the iOS share sheet.

  3. 03 Customize

    Make it your deck

    Preview the cards and name the deck. Keep categories as tags, split them into decks, or ignore them.

  4. 04 Study

    Start studying

    Tap Study Now, or open your deck later from My Decks.

Already have an export file? You can also import a supported TXT or XML file directly into Learn Chinese.
