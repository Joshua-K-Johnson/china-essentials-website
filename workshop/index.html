I had to figure China out as I went.

When I first arrived, everyday tasks felt harder than they should have. Which apps did I need? How would I pay? What happened during registration? How was I supposed to get from the airport to campus?

Years later, after studying and living in China and building China Essentials, I created this independent workshop so the next group of international students can arrive with answers—not avoidable anxiety.

— Joshua Johnson, founder of China Essentials

From international student to helping the next cohort.

Joshua is from Barbados, has spent years studying and living in China, and is a Tsinghua University PhD student in Shenzhen. He created China Essentials from lived experience—not a distant course outline.

06

Questions before you choose.

Still unsure? Email beta@chinaessentials.app.

Who is this for?+

International students preparing to attend any university in China, whether this is your first visit or your first time living here.

Is this a university workshop?+

No. This is an independent China Essentials workshop and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tsinghua University or any university.

Do I need to know Chinese?+

No. The workshop helps students prepare regardless of their current Mandarin level.

What happens after the workshop?+

Attendees receive groupchat and community access for follow-up questions, plus China Essentials app Beta and preparation resources.

How do I choose a date?+

Select one of the three sessions during registration. Both China Standard Time and Eastern Time are shown.

How long is the workshop?+

Each live session runs for 90 minutes, including the Live Q&A session.

How much does it cost?+

The workshop is a one-time $29 USD payment. There are no pricing tiers or recurring workshop charges.

What is China Essentials app Beta?+

It is early access to the China Essentials mobile app, including app guides, student and city resources, preparation tools, phrases and flashcards as available.
